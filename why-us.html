Meet Andile Mabuza

Welcome to Hair by Andz. We believe that your hair is your crown, and it deserves to be treated as such. Founded with a passion for flawless blends and premium quality, we source only the finest virgin hair extensions.

I started this brand because I understand that finding the perfect texture is a personal journey. Whether you are looking for the bouncy volume of our deep curls, the sleek elegance of straight black, or a bold statement with our signature meroon color, our collection is curated to empower your everyday look.

Every bundle is meticulously inspected to ensure minimal shedding, maximum longevity, and a natural blend. We are dedicated to providing you with an effortless, luxury experience from the moment you browse to the moment you install.
